Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Crowns is an offline puzzle game. It does not collect, send or share any personal data.

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Your progress (solved levels, times and settings) is stored only on your own device, in the browser's local storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data deletes it.
  • The app needs an internet connection only to download it the first time. After that it runs fully offline.
