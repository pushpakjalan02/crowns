How to play

  1. Place exactly one crown in every row, every column and every colour region.
  2. Two crowns can never touch each other, not even diagonally.
  3. Tap a cell once to mark it with × (meaning "no crown here"). Tap again for a crown. Tap a third time to clear it.
  4. Drag your finger across cells to mark many × at once.
  5. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone, without guessing.
